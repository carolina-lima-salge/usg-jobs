UGA vs Georgia State vs Georgia Tech: Which Institution Pays More for Administrative Roles?

If you're job hunting across the University System of Georgia's three flagship universities, salary is probably one of your first questions. The answer isn't always what people expect — and it varies significantly by role type.

We pulled salary data from all active job postings on USG Jobs as of May 2025 to give you a real, data-backed comparison. Here's what we found.

The Short Answer

Bottom line up front: Georgia Tech posts the highest average administrative salaries when salary data is available, followed closely by UGA. Georgia State posts the most jobs overall but publishes salary information for almost none of them — making it nearly impossible to compare directly.

What's Driving Georgia Tech's Higher Admin Salaries?

Georgia Tech's administrative salary average of $96,648 is almost $40,000 higher than UGA's $56,031 — a striking gap. Several factors explain this:

Tech-adjacent roles pull the average up. Georgia Tech's administrative staff frequently work alongside engineers and researchers. Roles like "Research Program Manager," "Grants Coordinator," and "Laboratory Administrator" command higher salaries at Tech than equivalent titles at comprehensive universities. In our data, the salary range for admin roles at GT ran from $60,000 to $175,000.

Smaller volume of postings. Georgia Tech posts far fewer administrative roles (232 total vs. UGA's 1,140), and fewer of those are entry-level. The postings that do include salary tend to skew toward senior roles.

Atlanta cost of living and private-sector competition. Georgia Tech competes with companies like Delta, NCR, Salesforce, and dozens of tech firms for the same talent. Salaries reflect that pressure.

Why UGA Tells a More Complete Story

UGA is the most useful institution for salary benchmarking because it publishes salary ranges on 60% of its job postings — far more than Tech (19%) or State (0%). With 688 postings showing compensation, you can actually build a realistic picture of what different roles pay.

At UGA, administrative salaries cluster between $40,000 and $75,000 for most staff roles, with outliers in IT management and director-level positions reaching $130,000–$170,000.

So Which Institution Should You Target?

The answer depends on what you're looking for:

If salary transparency matters to you: UGA is your best bet. Over half their postings list compensation upfront, which makes it much easier to know if a role is worth your time before applying.

If you're aiming for high administrative compensation: Georgia Tech's admin roles pay significantly more on average — particularly for anything grant-related, research operations, or technology-adjacent. Expect fierce competition and a more demanding role profile.

If you want sheer volume and variety: UGA has the most open positions by far — 1,140 at any given time — across virtually every administrative function. Georgia State has good volume but salary is opaque.

If location is a factor: Georgia State (Atlanta) and Georgia Tech (Atlanta) are metro. UGA is Athens — a significantly lower cost of living that can offset a lower headline salary, especially for housing.

What About the Other 23 USG Institutions?

Don't overlook the rest of the system. Kennesaw State, Augusta University, and Georgia Southern all post hundreds of roles and offer competitive salaries — particularly for administrative professionals who prefer suburban or smaller-city settings. Kennesaw State's admin average is $74,894, and Columbus State's is $79,262, both higher than UGA's $56,031 for comparable admin roles.
